The challenge · in 60 seconds

Self-driving & robot brains are too big and too hard to prove safe

Vision–Language–Action models (VLAMs) — 7–10 billion parameters — fuse cameras, sensors and language into driving and manipulation decisions. They're the strongest brains we have, and they cannot run on embedded hardware within a 100 ms decision budget, and nobody can formally certify a compressed version of one.

BOTTLENECK 01

Model footprint

A 7B model is ~14 GB fp16. An ECU has a fraction of that — and a heat budget.

BOTTLENECK 02

Attention cost

Attention is O(N²) in tokens. Real-time perception can't afford it.

BOTTLENECK 03

RL alignment

Teaching these models takes thousands of GPU-hours of rollouts.

BOTTLENECK 04

Safety guarantees

Cars need ISO 26262-level evidence. A compressed neural net has none.

The task: a reproducible quantum-enhanced / quantum-inspired component with a clear, measured advantage over a classical baseline — simulation allowed, every table reproducible.

Pillar A · compression with certificates

The weights are the proof

A tensor-train network stores each layer as a chain of small cores. Their spectral norms multiply into an exact global bound on how much the network can change inside any input region — the "this is provably safe here" box. No sampling. No data. A theorem.

L(W) = ∏ ‖Gᵢ‖₂ exact · computed from the cores alone · O(Σ rᵢ₋₁nᵢ²rᵢ) per matvec
THE COMPARISON

INT8 quantization

Error is data-dependent (clip ranges, calibration sets) and unfactorizable. There is no exact global constant — the certificate column cannot be computed for INT8, at any ratio. Ever.

THE COMPARISON

SVD truncation

Truncation error is distribution-free only in theory; the real loss is data-dependent — and SVD hands you no certificate object to compose into a safety argument.

Same accuracy, same ratio — one of us can print a safety guarantee, and it isn't them.
Pillar B · the commuting-Pauli compiler

Attention, but as forced mathematics

Cross-modal interaction tensors are written as sums of Pauli strings — the native algebra of quantum physics. Then the math is forced: group commuting terms, diagonalize each family with one Clifford, rewrite exactly. An identity, not an approximation.

1 · GROUP

Commuting families

Terms that commute get grouped (greedy coloring — polynomial). Family count m is measured, not assumed.

2 · DIAGONALIZE

One Clifford each

Each family is simultaneously diagonalized by a single Clifford. Standard VQE machinery, new job.

3 · REWRITE EXACTLY

T = Σⱼ Cⱼ†DⱼCⱼ

An identity. Drop a family? The cost is exactly ‖Σcₐ‖₁ — a printed, provable pruning certificate.

Already asked · already answered

The questions a judge will ask

"Your Lipschitz bound is loose."

Correct — and quantified. Every layer factor is the exact operator norm (power iteration on the tensor-train contraction, no dense matrix). The residual gap is measured as a tightness ratio κ at every Pareto point. SOS boxes close it locally.

"This is quantum-washing — Clifford circuits are classically simulable."

Agreed, and we claim no classical speedup from the Clifford step. The value is exactness, certifiable pruning, and the hardware pathway. The objection lands only on claims we explicitly don't make.

"QASA showed a classical bottleneck ties a PQC."

We cite it ourselves. That's why our claim is the compiler's exactness + certificates, not the encoding's power.

"Nothing is measured yet — why believe the tables?"

Every number is pre-registered with its kill criterion, its seeds, and its qicert.bench command. The N14 clean-env audit gates the whole report.
